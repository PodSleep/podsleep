PodSleep — install
==================

PodSleep is a new set of screens for Free Sleep on an Eight Sleep Pod. It is not
affiliated with or endorsed by Eight Sleep. Use at your own risk (see LICENSE.md).

Before you start
- Free Sleep is installed and working on your Pod (http://<pod-ip>:3000 opens).
- SSH to the Pod works on port 8822 (Free Sleep's scripts/setup_ssh.sh).
  Test it:  ssh -p 8822 root@<pod-ip> echo ok
- Your computer is on the same network as the Pod.

Mac or Linux
1. Open Terminal in this folder.
2. Run:  bash install-from-computer.sh
3. Enter your Pod's IP address when asked. It takes under a minute.

Windows 10 or 11
1. Open PowerShell in this folder.
2. Run:  powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\install-from-windows.ps1
3. Enter your Pod's IP address when asked.

Then open http://<pod-ip>:3000 on your phone. To add it to your home screen:
iPhone: Share > Add to Home Screen. Android (Chrome): menu > Add to Home screen.

After a Free Sleep update
Free Sleep updates replace PodSleep's files. Run the install again.

Removing PodSleep
Mac/Linux:  bash install-from-computer.sh uninstall
Windows:    powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\install-from-windows.ps1 uninstall
This puts Free Sleep's own screens back. Your schedules and settings are untouched.

If something goes wrong
The installer backs up Free Sleep's screens to /home/dac/podsleep-backup on the Pod,
and if Free Sleep doesn't restart after installing, it puts Free Sleep back by itself.
